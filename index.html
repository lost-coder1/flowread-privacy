FlowRead — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

FlowRead does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no analytics of any kind.

Data stored on your device

FlowRead stores your files, reading progress, and settings locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Network requests

FlowRead makes no network requests except in two specific cases:

  • Google Fonts — loaded once on first launch to display app fonts, then cached permanently on your device. No personal data is sent.
  • URL Reader (Pro feature) — when you paste an article URL, FlowRead fetches that URL directly from your device. No data about the request is sent to us. This feature is clearly labelled as requiring internet.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. FlowRead does not process or store any payment information.

Permissions

FlowRead requests storage access solely to let you select and read files from your device. No file content is transmitted anywhere.

Accessibility service

FlowRead includes an optional reading-reminder feature. If you turn it on, you choose which apps you want to be reminded in — for example a social media app you want to scroll less. To notice when one of those apps opens, FlowRead uses Android's accessibility service.

Usage access (optional)

If you grant usage access, FlowRead uses it for two things: to confirm you are still in the app before showing a reminder, so it does not interrupt you about an app you have already left, and to show you how long you have spent in that app today. This is read at the moment it is needed and immediately discarded. It is never stored or transmitted.

Anonymous usage events

FlowRead may send anonymous event pings — for example, that the app was opened or a book was downloaded. These contain no identifier of any kind: no account, no device ID, no file name, and nothing about what you read. They cannot be linked to you. No reading content, file name, or document ever leaves your device.

Children

FlowRead does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
